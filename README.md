## 📱 What Is openfpvcast?

openfpvcast is a free, open-source Android application that turns your phone into a powerful ground station for your DJI FPV goggles (model N3). Instead of being limited to the small screen inside the goggles, you can now:

- **Watch live video** from your drone directly on your phone
- **Record footage** with optional microphone audio from your phone
- **Control camera settings** without touching the goggles
- **Share your view** with friends on the same WiFi network

Think of it as a wireless bridge between your goggles and your phone—but even better, because it works over a simple USB cable.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🖥️ Live Video Streaming
Connect your DJI FPV goggles to your Android phone using a USB cable, and openfpvcast instantly displays the live camera feed on your phone's screen. The video is crisp and responsive, perfect for framing shots or monitoring your flight.

### 🎙️ Recording with Audio
Capture every moment of your flight directly to your phone's storage. You can choose to include audio from your phone's microphone, adding your voice commentary or ambient sounds to the recording.

### ⚙️ Camera Control
Adjust camera settings without fumbling with the goggles. Change resolution, frame rate, exposure, and other parameters right from your phone's touchscreen interface.

### 🌐 WebRTC Broadcasting
This is the standout feature! openfpvcast can re-broadcast your drone's live view over WebRTC technology. Anyone with a web browser connected to the same WiFi network can watch your flight in real-time. No special apps or installations needed—just a browser.

### 💾 Easy Recording Storage
All recordings are saved with clear filenames including timestamps, so you can easily find your best flights later.

## 🔧 System Requirements

### Android Device
- Operating System: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
- USB Port: Must support USB On-The-Go (OTG) functionality
- Storage Space: At least 200MB free for app and recordings
- Recommended: 4GB RAM or more for smooth video processing

### DJI FPV Goggles (N3)
- Model: DJI FPV Goggles N3 only
- Firmware: Updated to the latest version
- Battery: Fully charged before connection

### USB Connection
- Cable: USB-C to USB-C or USB-C to Micro-USB (whichever matches your phone)
- Ensure the cable supports data transfer, not just charging

### WiFi Network (for Broadcasting)
- A wireless router broadcasting on 2.4GHz or 5GHz
- Stable connection with reasonable bandwidth (at least 10Mbps upload recommended)

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### App Doesn't Detect Goggles
- Ensure the USB cable is fully inserted
- Try a different USB cable
- Check that you're using the correct goggles model (N3 only)
- Restart both your phone and the goggles
- Make sure your phone supports USB OTG

### Video Lag or Stuttering
- Close other apps running in the background
- Move closer to your WiFi router
- Ensure your phone has enough free storage space
- Reduce the video resolution in settings

### Broadcast Not Working
- Verify all devices are on the same network
- Check whether your router blocks peer-to-peer connections
- Try the 2.4GHz WiFi band instead of 5GHz
- Disable any VPN on your phone

### Recording Has No Audio
- Grant microphone permission to the app
- Check the audio toggle in the recording settings
- Speak close to your phone's microphone

## 💡 Pro Tips
